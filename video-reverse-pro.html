Video Reverse Pro – kaise karein?

Kisi video ki editing technique samajhna ho — kitne shots hain, cuts beat par hain ya nahi, rang aur roshni kaisi hai — to ye tool video ko shot-by-shot tor kar report bana deta hai. Creators is se kisi style ko seekh kar apni video dobara (remake) plan kar sakte hain.

  1. Video chunein (mp4, webm ya mov). Analysis aapke browser mein hi hota hai.
  2. Shots, har shot ka rang aur motion, BPM aur "cuts beat par" report dekhein.
  3. Chahein to Settings mein apni Gemini API key daal kar "Gemini se deep analysis" chalain — har shot ka verdict (filmed, AI, 3D, code) aur English remake prompt milega.
  4. Remake plan mein apne video model ki clip length set karein — shots utni generations mein batch ho jayenge. VIDEO.md download kar lein.

Aksar pooche jane wale sawal

Kya Video Reverse Pro free hai?
Haan, bilkul free hai. Sign-up ya account ki zaroorat nahi. Gemini wala step aapki apni free Gemini API key se chalta hai.

Kya meri video kisi server par upload hoti hai?
Nahi. Shots, rang aur BPM ka saara analysis aapke browser mein hota hai. Sirf jab aap apni Gemini key de kar deep analysis chalate hain, har shot ki ek tasveer (keyframe) Google ko jati hai — poori video kabhi nahi jati, aur key kahin save nahi hoti.

Bina Gemini key ke kya milta hai?
Shots ki list aur timing, har shot ka dominant rang aur motion, video ka BPM, kitne percent cuts beat par hain, poori video ki rang-birangi timeline, aur remake plan (bina prompts ke).

Kin videos par best chalta hai?
Shorts aur reels par best chalta hai. Bohat lambi video mein bohat tez cuts kabhi miss ho sakte hain, aur BPM audio ke pehle 90 seconds se naapta hai.

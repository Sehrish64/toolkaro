Watermark Remover – kaise karein?

Photo se koi chhota nishan, logo ya text saaf karein — brush se us hisse ko rang dein, tool aas paas ke rang aur pattern se us jagah ko bhar deta hai. ToolKaro ka ye tool free hai aur phone ya computer dono par chalta hai.

  1. Image chunein ya yahan drop karein (JPG, PNG ya WebP).
  2. Brush size set karke watermark wale hisse ko poora rang dein (laal nishan lagega).
  3. "Watermark hatao" dabayein, phir result save kar lein.
